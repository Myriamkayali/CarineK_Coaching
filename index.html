The Word Swap Method™

Catch the word. Name it. Swap it.

Most of what keeps you stuck isn’t a lack of effort. It’s a word. The ones you reach for automatically when things get hard quietly decide how you feel, whether you try again, and how fast you move on.

Book 1:1 coaching
1

Catch it

Notice the sentence while it’s still live in your head. Not later, in hindsight. In the moment.

2

Name the word

Find the one word doing the damage. Always. Never. Can’t. Failure. It’s usually smaller than you’d think.

3

Swap it

Trade it for a word that’s still honest, just forward-looking. Not a forced positive. A door left open.

Worked example

“I always mess this up.”

Swap

“I’ve messed this up before, and I’m still learning what works.”

“Always” wasn’t accurate, and it closed the door on change. The swap took about two weeks of repeating before it felt true.

Psychologists call this cognitive reframing. Decades of research back it. The Word Swap Method just makes it small enough to use mid-moment.

The swap is the entry point, not the whole fix. Underneath the one word are patterns you’ve been running for years, the kind that are hard to see from the inside. Rebuilding those into a script you can actually use under pressure is the coaching work.
