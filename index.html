About Carine

Real change happens when insight becomes action.

Registered Nurse Nursing Educator Certified Professional Coach

Years spent supporting people through some of their hardest moments taught Carine one important lesson: most people already know what they should do. What they struggle with is how to apply it consistently, when life gets busy, stressful, or overwhelming.

That realization inspired the creation of the Kayali WAVES™ Framework, a coaching approach designed to build practical skills, greater self-awareness, and sustainable habits through structured guidance and meaningful action.

"Real change happens when insight becomes action."

Off the clock, Carine's own reset looks a lot like her coaching philosophy in practice. An early walk by the sea, a quiet stretch of mountain air, a morning that starts before the noise does. Small, repeatable rituals. The same idea she brings into every session.

The Method

The Kayali WAVES™ Framework

Five stages, moved through together, each one building the practical skills that make up your Mental First Aid Kit™.

W

Welcome Awareness

Recognize patterns without judgment.

A

Assess What Matters

Clarify priorities, strengths, values, and goals.

V

Visualize Possibilities

Develop practical strategies and meaningful solutions.

E

Empower Through Action

Create realistic action plans and build confidence through practice.

S

Sustain Growth

Develop habits and tools that keep serving you after coaching ends.

Because confidence isn't built by waiting for life to become easier. It's built by learning how to navigate every wave that comes your way.
